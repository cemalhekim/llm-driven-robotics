\underline{Guidelines for Execution}\\
\begin{enumerate}
\item The work shall be carried out in consultation with the supervisor. It may not be published or made available to third parties without the consent of the institute.
\item If machines and equipment of the Department of IAT are to be used, they may only be operated during working hours, or exceptionally beyond these hours with the supervisor’s approval. In any case, for safety reasons, at least one other person must be within visual or calling distance.
\item Before commissioning machines and equipment, the safety report must be studied. A current version is available at the machine and with the supervising staff member.
\end{enumerate}

\underline{Guidelines for Documentation} \\
\begin{enumerate}
\item A one-page abstract shall precede the thesis. It must include: topic, student’s name, reference number from the task description, submission date, objectives of the work, and essentially the results of the work.
\item In addition to the printed version, both a Microsoft Word and an Adobe PDF version must be submitted.
\item Prior to submission, the student must provide the supervisor with all files generated during the course of the work (results, evaluations, graphics, simulation models, etc.). The compatibility of the software used must be agreed with the supervisor before starting the work.
\end{enumerate}

\underline{Supervision Feedback Questionnaire}\\ \\
After receiving the grade, a questionnaire shall be used to evaluate the supervision at the IAT in order to provide suggestions for improving the content offered as well as the equipment of the department.
