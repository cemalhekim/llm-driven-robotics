\begin{vorwort}{Preface}
There is an endless amount of literature on \LaTeX, typography, scientific typesetting, and related topics. The present small document is intended as a supplement for writing dissertations or other reports, diploma theses, etc., at the Department of Industrial Automation Technology of the Technical University of Berlin, and was created from a template originally designed by Martin Ruskowski for dissertations published by the VDI publishing house.  

This hopefully useful collection of small tips for writing a dissertation makes no claim to completeness, correctness, or providing a perfect solution. Rather, it is meant to serve as guidance and a quick start, and is explicitly intended to encourage extensions, improvements, and modifications. Many aspects are subjective in nature; one may or may not like this document. Ultimately, however, most elements should be easily adaptable to individual needs, although a more or less uniform layout is certainly desirable.  

In addition to dissertations, research reports, diploma, master’s, bachelor’s, project, or laboratory reports can be produced without difficulty. For this purpose, the same \LaTeX\ document class {\tt iat.cls} is always used, which can be called with various options that mainly influence the layout of the title page. I have named this version of {\tt iat.cls} as 3.3, as I have continually modified it. This is now the first documented version and thus released for review: any suggestions for improvements, as well as reports of minor or major errors, are welcome!  

Incidentally, this document is structured exactly as a dissertation could be, with the exception of the title page; otherwise, for example, some parts would make little sense here.  
\\Enjoy working with {\LaTeX}!
\vfill
Cemal Hekim \hfill Berlin, \today

\end{vorwort}
